**People** is per team. On **All**, the screen asks you to **Select a Team**.

</Frame>

**Owner** and **Admin (Team)** see **Invite member**. **User** and **Developer** do not.

The dialog asks for an email and a role. The roles are **Owner**, **Admin (Team)**, **User**, and **Developer**. Only an **Owner** is offered the **Owner** option.

- **Owner.** Full control of the team: invite and remove members, and edit all permissions and API tokens.
- **Admin (Team).** Invite members and edit account permissions. No access to platform features (Owner only).
- **User.** Works only with the assigned permissions. Cannot invite or edit others.
- **Developer.** Same as User; aimed at integrations with API access.

<Frame caption="Invite member dialog">
  <img

After you send the invite, it stays under **Pending invites**, with **Cancel**.

The person who opens the link sees **Team invitation** and **Accept invitation**. Without an account, they see **Create account** or **Sign in**, using the invited email.
