title: "My access"
description: "See your permissions and request one you do not have"
icon: "key"
---

**My access** is in the sidebar.

Each card is a team: the name, **Your role**, and the **People** link.

Two blocks: **Platform access** and **Account permissions**. A granted permission is shown as active. One you do not have is requested with **Request this permission from Owners**.

When you send it, the notice is **Permission request sent to {team} Owners**. If it was already requested, **This permission is already pending approval** and the label changes to **Requested**.

With no memberships, the screen says **No team memberships**.
