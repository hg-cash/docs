---
title: "Member permissions"
description: "Edit what a member can do on the active team"
icon: "shield"
---

An **Owner** or an **Admin (Team)** opens **Member permissions** from **Permissions** on **People**.

**Platform access** is edited by the **Owner**. An **Admin (Team)** sees it as read-only and can edit **Account permissions**.

If the member is an **Owner**, the screen says they have full access and there are no checkboxes.

**Save changes** asks you to **Confirm permissions**.
