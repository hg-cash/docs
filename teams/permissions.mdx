---
title: "Member permissions"
description: "Edit what a member can do on the active team"
icon: "shield"
---

To change what another person on the team can do, an **Owner** or an **Admin (Team)** goes to **People**, selects the member, and opens **Permissions**. That opens **Member permissions** for the active team.

There are two blocks:

- **Platform access.** Edited by the **Owner**. An **Admin (Team)** can view it, but cannot edit it.
- **Account permissions.** Both the **Owner** and the **Admin (Team)** can edit them.

If the member is an **Owner**, the screen says they have full access to the team and does not let you edit permissions one by one.

When you finish, **Save changes** opens **Confirm permissions** so you can review the list before applying.
