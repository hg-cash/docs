title: "Invite members"
description: "Invite a person to the active team"
icon: "user-plus"
---

The **People** screen lists the members of the active team (the one at the top left of the sidebar). If it is on **All**, the app does not list members: it asks you to **Select a Team** before you continue.

**Invite member** appears only if you are an **Owner** or **Admin (Team)** of that active team. People with the **User** or **Developer** role cannot invite.

For example, María (maria@acme.com) is **Owner** of Acme Corp and **User** of Partner X. With Acme Corp active, she sees the button. With Partner X, or on **All**, it is not there.

Clicking it opens a dialog for the invitee’s email and role. The roles are **Owner**, **Admin (Team)**, **User**, and **Developer**. Only someone who is already an **Owner** of the team is offered the **Owner** option.

- **Owner.** Full control of the team: invite and remove members, and edit all permissions and API tokens.
- **Admin (Team).** Invite members and edit account permissions. No access to platform features (Owner only).
- **User.** Works only with the assigned permissions. Cannot invite or edit others.
- **Developer.** Same as User; aimed at integrations with API access.

After you send the invite, it stays under **Pending invites**. From there you can **Cancel** one that has not been accepted yet.

The invited person opens the link from the email and sees **Team invitation**. If they already have an account, they can **Accept invitation**. If not, they choose **Create account** or **Sign in** with the same invited email.
