---
title: "Member permissions"
description: "Edit what a member can do on the team you have selected"
icon: "shield"
---

To change what another person on the team can do, an **Owner** or an **Admin (Team)** goes to **People**, selects the member, and opens **Permissions**. That opens **Member permissions** for the team you have selected.

There are two blocks:

- **Platform access.** Edited by the **Owner**. An **Admin (Team)** can view it, but cannot edit it.
- **Account permissions.** Both the **Owner** and the **Admin (Team)** can edit them.

If the member is an **Owner**, the screen says they have full access to the team and does not let you edit permissions one by one.

When you finish choosing what to grant or revoke, click **Save changes**. Before anything is applied, **Confirm permissions** opens with a summary of the changes so you can review them.

If a member requested a permission from **My access**, on **People** you see a pending-requests indicator on their row. When you open **Permissions**, those requests are marked **Requested**. You can grant them when you save, or **Dismiss** a request.

**Owners** get a notification in the bell (**Pending permission request**) with a link to that editor. When you save permission changes, the member gets **Permissions updated**. If you dismiss a request, that person gets **Permission request dismissed**.
