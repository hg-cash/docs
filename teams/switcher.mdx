title: "Switch team"
description: "The header shows the active team"
icon: "arrow-right-arrow-left"
---

The header shows the active team.

With one team, a menu does not open.

With two or more teams, the menu is called **Switch Team**. It lists **All** (label **All Teams**) and each team with its role: **Owner**, **Admin (Team)**, **User**, or **Developer**.
