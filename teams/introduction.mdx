A **team** groups the people and accounts you work with on HG.cash. It is how you organize who can enter the dashboard and what they can do on each account in the team.

Each person on a team has one role: **Owner**, **Admin**, **User**, or **Developer**. The role sets how far someone can manage the team; the detailed permissions are shown and requested in **My access**, and edited from **People**.

When you belong to at least one team, the top left shows the active team name and your role on that team. In the sidebar you will find **My access** and **People**: from there you review your permissions and invite or manage members. The following pages in this section walk through each one.
