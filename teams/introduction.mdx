A **team** groups the people and accounts you work with on HG.cash. It is how you organize who joins and what they can do on each account in the team.

Each person on a team has one role: **Owner**, **Admin**, **User**, or **Developer**. The role sets how far someone can manage the team; the detailed permissions are shown and requested in **My access**, and edited from **People**.

When you belong to at least one team, the top left shows the team you have selected and your role on that team. In the sidebar you will find **My access** and **People**: from there you review your permissions and invite or manage members. The following pages in this section walk through each one.

If you belong to more than one team, you can change the selected team at the top left or choose **All**. With one team selected, what you see in the rest of the app (for example Home, Accounts, or Transactions) is limited to that team. With **All**, you see the unified view across your teams. To see members or send invites you need a team selected at the top left. **All** is not enough: choose the team first.
